# Brand: Garden Classic, Court Geometry symbol

## Palette

| Name | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jersey Cream | `#F7F4EE` | page, text on navy |
| Garden Navy | `#17365D` | court geometry, controls, primary buttons |
| Basketball Copper | `#B94B21` | the ball, the answer, focus rings |

Text uses `--ink` `#132A47`, a deep shade of Garden Navy. Every other colour in the UI is a tint or shade of these three. The tokens live in `src/styles.css`, and the canvas share cards mirror them in `src/ui/share-card.ts`.

**Contrast** (WCAG):

| Pair | Ratio |
| --- | ---: |
| Navy on cream | 11.1:1 |
| Copper on cream | 4.7:1 |
| Cream on navy | 11.1:1 |
| Soft text `#4E5F73` on cream | 6.0:1 |
| Light copper `#F4B08E` (answer on the navy field) | 6.7:1 |

Copper on navy is only 2.4:1, so copper never carries meaning on navy without a cream or navy outline.

## Symbol

The canonical artwork is `src/brand/symbol.ts`. It shows the scoring end of an NBA half court, seen from above:
- The baseline
- The three-point line: corner lines 3 ft in from the sidelines, and an arc of 23 ft 9 in around the basket centre
- The lane: 16 ft wide and 19 ft deep

Everything is drawn at one uniform scale from `src/engine/court.ts`. A copper ball sits on the lane's axis, just outside the arc, on its way in. It is the game's run toward the basket.

**Rules:**
- The artwork is the symbol only. It never contains lettering, initials, numbers, gradients, shadows or filters. A test enforces this.
- The product name is always set separately, as text: `Stat Window` in DM Sans.
- Navy is for the court; copper is only for the ball.
- On navy, use the cream court. For one-colour printing, use `symbol-mono.svg` (`currentColor`).

The symbol is simplified for 16 px. The rim, backboard and free-throw circle merge into the arc below about 48 px, so they are left out at every size and the mark stays the same everywhere.

## Exploration

Nine refinements were explored and compared at real pixel sizes of 16, 24, 32, 48 and 64. The comparison sheets are in the project files under `stat-window/brand/explore/`.

| Variant | What was tried | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Full half court in a frame | Turned to mud below 32 px |
| B, D | Thin lines, a navy tile, the free-throw circle | The circle merged with the arc at 16–24 px |
| C | The arc alone | Legible, but no longer read as basketball |
| G | A painted (solid) lane | Read as a face or shield |
| H | Horizontal, matching the in-game court strip | Read as the letter E |
| **K (chosen)** | Vertical scoring end, open lane, ball outside the arc | Distinct at every size, in mono and on navy |
