# V5.1 statistics definitions

One foundation, `src/engine/history.ts`, feeds the home, the Weekly Scoreboard (`weekly.ts`) and the Scouting Report (`scouting.ts`).

## Eligible records
A saved daily run counts only when all hold:
- It is an official daily record. Practice, untimed practice, tutorial and challenge-only scores are separate stores and never count.
- Its day is a real puzzle day in the current season (`inSeason`). Beta and other seasons are excluded, so s1 starts empty.
- It is not in the future.
- It is a finished five-round run (advanced and saved results both equal five).
- Its saved windows verify against the published slate with the game's scoring rules (`verifiedDailyRounds`). Tampered, stale or incomplete records are not counted. A finished run that cannot be loaded is shown as unavailable, never as zero.

## Weekly Scoreboard
- Weeks run Monday to Sunday on the Eastern puzzle-day calendar (DST-safe, date arithmetic on ISO days, not timestamps).
- Per day states: completed (including a real 0), unplayed (elapsed, no eligible game), today open, upcoming, before launch (midweek season start; not counted as missed), unavailable.
- Total is the sum of completed days (max 3,500). Average is total over completed games, shown only with at least one. Best day is the highest completed score.
- Current and previous eligible weeks only; weeks wholly before launch are not offered. No global leaderboard.
- The share text is spoiler-free: Stat Window identity, exact week dates, daily scores or missing states, total, completed count and an invitation. No answers, players or questions.

## Scouting Report
- Metrics: completed games, average and best game, streaks, catch rate, points per attempt, recent against earlier comparable play.
- Outcomes per attempt: scoring hit (points above 0), zero-point catch (inside the window, 0 points), miss, timeout. Catch rate = (scoring hits + zero-point catches) / attempts.
- Recent vs earlier needs PERIOD_GAMES (7) games in each period. A difference is called "more/fewer" only at 10 or more points per game (PERIOD_DIFFERENCE).
- Risk profile uses `potentialPoints()` of each saved window. Bands: Conservative 0 to 29, Balanced 30 to 69, High potential 70 to 100. A band conclusion needs MIN_CATEGORY_ATTEMPTS (5) attempts; windows are called wider or narrower at 5 average potential points of difference.
- Knowledge profile groups by question meta: format, era, family or context label, player vs team. Missing meta is shown as explicit "Unknown", never guessed. A category with fewer than 5 attempts shows descriptive counts only; the highest-scoring category must lead the runner-up by 5 points per attempt.
- Language is descriptive, never causal. Near miss = a miss within 5% of the scale.
- Empty and small histories show an honest state, not blank charts. Charts carry text equivalents.

## Events
`scouting_*`, `weekly_*` and practice-discovery events carry only bounded enums and counts (docs/MEASUREMENT.md).
