## Statistical definitions (D, F, G)

Full definitions: [V5.1-STATS.md](V5.1-STATS.md) and [PERCENTILES.md](PERCENTILES.md). In short:

- History, streaks, weekly and scouting count only current-season, verified, timed official runs. Practice, Survival, QA and the tutorial are excluded.
- Per-question crowd lines appear from 100 eligible games. A scoring round gets its midrank percentile ("Xth percentile for this question", never "beat X%"), shown in steps of 5 and between the 5th and 95th. A zero-point round gets the crowd's scoring share instead. Shares are withheld when a cell or its complement has fewer than 5 games, and the percentile table is withheld when the zero or scoring cell holds 1–4 games.

## Redis costs (G)

Outcome counts are added by the same Lua script that stores the total, so there is no new request type. Upper bounds are charged before work and checked on real Redis (`server/redis.test.ts`).

| Path | Cost |
|---|---|
| First completion, with questions | 27 |
| Duplicate resend | 14 |
| DELETE, upper bound (9 days × beta, s1, QA) | 387 |
| One network per day, worst case | 1,080 of 14,000 |

The read ceiling (4,000 a day) is the uncertain part: per-region edge caching means busy multi-region days can pause the crowd lines for the rest of that UTC day. Scores and gameplay are unaffected and spending stays capped.

## Day 1 restart (R)

The launch day is committed in `src/engine/launch-config.ts`, one value for the client and the score API. Guards:

- CI `season-check`: the day must be at least 2 days ahead (Eastern), on or after `VARIETY_FROM`, with at least 28 official slates. A started season can't move. A push to main is checked against the commit before it.
- The production build refuses any deploy that would switch a begun season on mid-day: it builds only if production already serves that launch day. It also refuses the preview switch.
- Tabs opened before the activation deploy reload at the midnight rollover.
- Emergency rollback is a committed acknowledgement (`SEASON_ROLLBACK_FROM`). DELETE searches every season regardless.

Runbook: [LAUNCH.md](LAUNCH.md).
