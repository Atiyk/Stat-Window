# Stat Window V5.2 release report

V5.2 (branch `statwindow/v5-2`, from `866902e`, the V5.1 merge) changes what players see in a question, not how they play it: five questions, two brackets, the same scoring and the 24-second clock. It replaces seven question formats with three, re-edits every official slot to a 2/2/1 mix, simplifies the home, round and results screens, and adds a weekly scorecard image.

## Taxonomy v2: three player formats

`src/content/formats.ts` (`TAXONOMY_VERSION = 2`):

| Format | What the player sees |
|---|---|
| `recall` | A numerical question on its own. A computed answer (a gap, a share, a sum, a rank) is still recall. |
| `reference` | The prompt prints a related verified figure that helps the estimate. |
| `missing-point` | A 3 to 7 bar chart of related verified observations with one gap: the answer. |

`difference`, `ratio`, `ranking` and `trajectory` are retired (`RETIRED_FORMATS`). They stay in the storage schema because published beta days, saved runs and the Survival reserve keep their labels. In the guide `guideFor(retired)` falls back to recall, and the Scouting Report lists retired formats separately under their own names.

**The 2/2/1 rule.** From `VARIETY_FROM` (2026-11-30) every official slate has two questions of one format, two of another and one of the third, and no retired format. No official day repeats the previous day's format sequence. `slateFormatIssues` and `sequenceIssues` implement it; `validateNba`, `content:check`, the factory's `selectSlate` and the "V5.2 official-season audit" tests in `scripts/nba/formats.test.ts` enforce it. Details: [QUESTION-FACTORY.md](QUESTION-FACTORY.md#question-formats-and-the-221-rule-nba-taxonomy-v2-from-v52).

## Player-facing changes

From the commit messages of `4257ffb`, `9117b9f`, `3c5b15e`, `a3938a5` and `15ea7cc`:
- **Home:** "How well do you know ball?" with a static or lightly animated wide-versus-tight window demo (points from the real scoring formula, `WindowDemo.tsx`), a primary "Play Game N" from the season config, and, for new visitors, "Try a practice question first", which opens the untimed Warriors walkthrough.
- **Guide:** one worked example per player format (recall, reference, missing-point); retired-format lessons removed.
- **Format cue:** one instruction line the first time a player meets a reference or missing-point question, gone once met. The "?" stays on every question; the "New type" badge is gone.
- **Reveal:** the answer first, then caught or not with points, then "Your window"; the reaction, note and fun fact follow, the crowd line comes last, and Next never waits.
- **Missing-point chart:** 3:1 bars, a stronger dashed gap, the answer fading and growing in (respecting reduced motion), labels readable at 320 px, and "Bars start at 0" in the text equivalent.
- **Mobile:** 44 px minimum bracket targets with an extended hit area, a numeric or decimal keypad per scale, decimal-comma entry, horizontal safe-area insets.
- **Results:** the total out of 500 and a verdict lead, then the court run and answer time. Sharing the game just played always comes first; past games, the week strip and other sections sit in folds below, with quiet links to the Weekly Scoreboard, Scouting Report and other modes.
- **Weekly Scoreboard:** a scorecard image (square or portrait) with preview, "Save image" and, where supported, "Share image".
- **Scouting Report:** a headline profile (one line per question a scout would ask, each with its sample or "not yet") and a game-log chart of recent totals.

## Statistical definitions

Full definitions: [V5.1-STATS.md](V5.1-STATS.md).
- **Weekly card states** (`weeklyCard()`): each day carries a mark, a value and a word, so nothing depends on colour: scored (points and bar), played zero ("0", "played"), unplayed ("–", "not played"), today, to come, not open (before launch) and unavailable ("?"). The total is shown against the eligible maximum, which counts only season days in the week.
- **Scouting profile** (`buildReport().profile`): recent average (last 7 games), catches out of attempts, best game, strongest statistic category (sample-gated), risk profile (needs 5 windows), consistency as the middle half of totals (needs 5 games) and trend (needs 14 games, 10-point threshold). Format rows split into the three player formats and earlier retired formats.
