# NBA fact audit, 2026-11-16

Scope: all 250 scheduled questions in `data/nba/calendar.json` (50 days). Three auditors each took about a third of the calendar. A separate critic drew its own random sample without seeing their choices (see `docs/QA.md`, NBA release).

## Method
- **Offline, every question:** prompt and context against the stored quote (who, season, regular season vs playoffs vs Finals vs one game, per game vs total, franchise identity, traded players); units; scale; arithmetic of every derivation; superlatives ("record", "most", "first", "ever", "never", "only") and present-tense claims; stat eras (blocks and steals 1973–74, turnovers 1977–78, threes 1979–80); spoilers within a day and from earlier days.
- **Online, every question:** each auditor fetched every source page in their third (109 article fetches in all, plus 9 cross-check pages such as Stephen Curry, Klay Thompson, Dwight Howard, Bill Russell and Wilt Chamberlain's career achievements). The fetch tool returns at most about 125 characters of verbatim text per quote, so for long sentences only the clause with the number was matched word for word.
- Limits: the session's shared fetch budget (100 an hour) ran out before a few second-source checks (listed under "Residual uncertainty").

### Quarantined
`nba-lebron11-4q-total` (above), `nba-celtics24-ortg` (offensive rating differs by stats provider and the "record" may be stale; replaced by `nba-brown24-finals-ppg`, re-rated hard), `nba-lively24-fg` (rookie record likely ignores league minimums and his own article states no record; replaced by `nba-spurs24-losing-streak`, re-rated expert). Full records are kept in `data/nba/quarantine.json` and listed in `data/nba/rejected.json`. None were rewritten into new facts.

### Majors, in short
Stale or unsupported records reworded to "then", "at the time" or dropped: Curry's 157-game three streak (he later reached 268), Boston's 19 threes (only the nine in a quarter is a record), Golden State's 20 Finals threes, Shaq's 39 free-throw attempts (Howard 39 in 2012), the 2014 Spurs' margin, the 1966–67 Sixers' 125 points a game and their tied 46–4 start, the Lakers' 68-point "franchise low", Mazzulla "tying Bill Russell" (Russell was 34 in 1968), the 2024 Celtics' "second only to the 2017 Warriors" (needs "since four best-of-seven rounds"), Boston's 26 playoff games and 39-point clincher. Prompts that gave the answer away (1959 to 1966 is eight titles) or let earlier days give it away (Allen's and Curry's Finals three records) were rewritten. Scales fixed where the prompt capped the answer (Mike Miller took eight threes; Kerr's ninth title). The Bobcats' 23-game streak context and the 2016 Warriors–Rockets 88-attempt game were made answerable.

### Accepted minors
- Answers not on the step grid (Oscar's 899 assists with step 5 and three others): a window can still contain the answer, so scoring is fair; left as is.
- Recaps restate their own day's answers. They are shown only after round 5 (`src/ui/Results.tsx`), never during play or in a share; a test would need to change if that ever moves.
- `nba-elmore73-half` and `nba-bol85-quarter`: records since tied; the prompts don't claim they stand alone.

## Residual uncertainty (not resolved in this run)
- Second sources not fetched for: the 1953–54 Lakers playoff scores (the 68-point claim was removed instead), and `nba-celtics08-playoff-games` / `nba-celtics08-g6-margin` against the 2025 and 2026 Finals (reworded to "then" so they stay true either way).
- Partial verbatim matches only: `nba-jazz96-comeback`, `nba-bulls96-home-streak`, `nba-rockets94-start`, `nba-wilt62-ppg`, `nba-kidd02-finals-td`, `nba-sixers67-ppg`.
- The three reserve facts moved into the schedule were checked in the branch's earlier quote recheck; this run's critic rechecks them online.
- Every source is a single Wikipedia article. Wikipedia pages can be wrong (two were found wrong here). High-traffic claims were cross-checked against a second Wikipedia article; no non-Wikipedia source was used, by design (see `docs/NBA_DATA_RIGHTS.md`).
