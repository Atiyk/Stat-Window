How well do you know ball?

Stat Window is a free daily NBA trivia game. Every answer is a number. Instead of guessing it exactly, choose a range that you think contains it.

How the game works

  1. Read the basketball question and look at the numerical scale.
  2. Move the two brackets to choose your lowest and highest guesses. The potential points change as your window narrows.
  3. Lock your window before the 24-second shot clock expires.
  4. If the answer is inside your window, you score based on its width. If you miss, you score zero. A very wide correct range can also be worth zero.
  5. Repeat for all five questions. Your maximum total is 500 points.

Why tighter windows score more

On an illustrative scale from 0 to 100, suppose the answer is 66. A window from 20 to 80 catches it for 16 points; a window from 60 to 72 catches it for 79. A window from 60 to 65 misses and scores zero. The answer can sit on either boundary. These are examples of the scoring mechanic, not daily puzzle answers.

Three types of NBA questions

  • Recall: estimate a historical basketball statistic.
  • Reference: use a related statistic to help reason toward the answer.
  • Missing point: estimate the missing value in a chart.

Frequently asked questions

When does the game reset?

A new daily game starts at midnight US Eastern time. Everyone gets the same five questions.

Is it free?

Yes. No account is required. Your game history stays in this browser; the optional score comparison and play counts are described in our privacy notice.

Can I play with friends?

After finishing, share your score or challenge link. Friends can open the same puzzle and compare results. You can also view your weekly record.

Can I practice?

The game offers an untimed walkthrough for learning the brackets. Previous official games become available in the practice archive as the season progresses.
