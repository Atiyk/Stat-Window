NBA trivia questions and answers

How well do you know NBA basketball? Try eight questions about the rules, scoring and game clock. Each answer has a short explanation and an official rulebook reference.

Play today's five NBA questions

  1. How long is an NBA regulation quarter?

    Reveal answer

    12 minutes. Four such periods make 48 minutes of regulation game time. NBA rulebook source.

  2. How many minutes does an NBA overtime period last?

    Reveal answer

    5 minutes. Additional overtime periods are played if the score stays tied. NBA rulebook source.

  3. How many seconds are on a standard new-possession shot clock?

    Reveal answer

    24 seconds. Certain situations have different reset rules. NBA rulebook source.

  4. After a missed shot hits the rim and the offense regains possession, what is the shot-clock reset?

    Reveal answer

    14 seconds. The shortened reset gives the offense less time on its second opportunity. NBA rulebook source.

  5. How many players per team normally play at once?

    Reveal answer

    5 players. Two teams therefore put ten players on the floor. NBA rulebook source.

  6. How many personal fouls normally disqualify an NBA player?

    Reveal answer

    6 fouls. The rulebook describes rare exceptions when a team has no eligible substitutes. NBA rulebook source.

  7. How much is a made free throw worth?

    Reveal answer

    1 point. Free throws and field goals are scored differently. NBA rulebook source.

  8. If a shooter makes a field goal with a foot on the three-point line, how many points count?

    Reveal answer

    2 points. A three-point attempt must originate outside the arc under the scoring rule. NBA rulebook source.
