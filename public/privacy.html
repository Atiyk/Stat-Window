Privacy

Stat Window · last updated 22 November 2026

What Stat Window collects

Stat Window has no accounts, no ads, no tracking cookies and no third-party marketing pixels. Your game history (rounds, scores, streaks, settings) is saved in your own browser (localStorage) and never uploaded.

We do not sell or share this data, build profiles, track you across sites, or fingerprint your device. A browser is not a person: we cannot link your phone and laptop, and we never claim to.

Global rankings (not enabled yet)

Joining the public global leaderboard is a separate explicit choice. The game would send your five locked windows and a random browser code to re-score the official puzzle. The server would keep a pseudonymous score linked to a salted hash of that code until 30 days after each day, week, month or 120-day season ranking period ends. Linked daily records are partitioned into 120-day blocks and expire after their last potentially retained ranking; continued play does not extend older blocks; expiry follows New York calendar midnights including daylight-saving changes; other players could see your generated nickname and score in day, week, month and season standings. You could leave and request deletion of all still-retained linked leaderboard entries from the results screen. After successful deletion, a hashed revocation marker with no score or raw browser code would remain for 180 days to reject delayed submissions from old tabs. Choose Leave before clearing this site's data: clearing browser storage first loses the random code needed to remove those entries, so they may stay publicly listed until their normal expiry. A failed or rate-limited deletion must be retried and is not a successful deletion. The feature is currently disabled pending privacy, cost and deletion review. No leaderboard scores are being collected.

Your choices

  • Turn either off any time: the "Privacy and your data" button on the results screen, or "Privacy choices" in How it works. Turning analytics off takes effect immediately.
  • Global Privacy Control / Do Not Track: if your browser sends either signal, both start off. Analytics stays off; the results screen asks before adding your score. You can still switch either on yourself.
  • EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland: your score is only added after you tap "Count my score" (the server checks the country of the request, which it does not store). If the location is unknown we ask too. Before you answer, the app sends only today's date to ask whether a yes is needed: no code is created and your windows are not sent.
  • Delete my scores: removes every score your browser sent that is still linked to its code (the last 7 days), then replaces the code and turns the daily comparison off, so the same days aren't sent again. You can tick it back on. Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes your local history and the code.
  • Gameplay never depends on either: if they are blocked, offline or off, the game works the same.

Children

Stat Window is a general-audience sports game and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no names, emails or contact details from anyone.
